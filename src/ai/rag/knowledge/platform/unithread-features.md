# uniThread Features

## Overview

uniThread provides CRM functionality for organizing customer information, managing sales opportunities, tracking activities, and coordinating customer-related work.

The platform connects customer records and workflows so users can manage relationships throughout the customer lifecycle.

## Leads

Leads represent potential customers or business opportunities.

Users can use lead management to:

- Capture and organize potential customers
- Track lead information
- Qualify potential opportunities
- Assign leads to team members
- Monitor lead progress
- Move leads through a workflow

Lead management helps teams organize prospects before they become customers or active sales opportunities.

## Contacts

Contacts store information about people associated with customer relationships.

Contact management may include:

- Contact details
- Communication information
- Notes
- Related activities
- Associated leads, deals, or customers
- Assigned team members

Contacts help users maintain a centralized record of people and their relationship history.

## Deals

Deals represent sales opportunities that an organization is pursuing.

Deal management supports:

- Creating and tracking sales opportunities
- Organizing deals in a sales pipeline
- Monitoring deal progress
- Associating deals with contacts or customers
- Tracking relevant deal information
- Managing sales stages

A sales pipeline provides a visual way to understand where opportunities are in the sales process.

## Sales Pipeline

The sales pipeline organizes deals according to their current stage.

Example stages include:

- Prospecting
- Proposal
- Negotiation
- Closed Won
- Closed Lost

Pipeline stages represent the progress of an opportunity from initial interest to a completed or unsuccessful outcome.

The exact stages and configuration available to a user depend on the application's implementation and organization settings.

## Customers

Customers represent established customer relationships.

Customer management helps users continue tracking information and interactions after a lead or deal progresses into a customer relationship.

Customer records may be connected to:

- Contacts
- Deals
- Activities
- Notes
- Communication history

## Tasks

Tasks represent work that needs to be completed.

Task management supports workflows such as:

- Creating tasks
- Assigning tasks to team members
- Setting due dates
- Tracking task status
- Managing follow-up work
- Organizing CRM-related responsibilities

Tasks help teams ensure that customer-related actions and follow-ups are not overlooked.

## Notes

Notes allow users to record useful information related to CRM records and activities.

Notes can be used for:

- Customer details
- Meeting notes
- Follow-up information
- Sales strategies
- Important relationship context
- Internal reminders

Notes help preserve information that may be useful during future customer interactions.

## Activities

Activities represent customer-related actions or interactions.

They can help teams maintain a history of work performed around leads, contacts, deals, and customers.

Activity records may include information about:

- The related CRM record
- The type of activity
- The activity description
- The responsible user
- The date or time of the activity

The exact activity types depend on the application's available functionality.

## Messaging

uniThread includes messaging functionality for customer-related communication and internal collaboration.

Messaging may include:

- Internal team conversations
- Customer-related communication
- Conversation history
- Messages associated with CRM workflows

Communication capabilities may differ depending on the channel and the current product status.

## Organizations and Members

uniThread supports organizations and organization members.

Organization management helps teams:

- Maintain a shared CRM workspace
- Manage members
- Assign roles
- Control access to organization data
- Coordinate CRM operations within a workspace

An organization represents a workspace boundary for shared CRM activity and data.

## User Management

User management supports the administration of members and their access within an organization.

Depending on the user's role and permissions, available operations may include:

- Viewing organization members
- Inviting members
- Managing member access
- Assigning or managing roles
- Controlling access to CRM functionality

Actual permissions are determined by the application's authorization rules.

## Metrics and Dashboard Information

uniThread provides CRM-related dashboard information and metrics to help users understand activity within their workspace.

Depending on the available dashboard functionality, this may include:

- Lead counts
- Contact counts
- Deal counts
- Customer counts
- Pipeline information
- Recent activities
- Business statistics
- Charts or summarized metrics

Dashboard results should be interpreted according to the user's authorized organization context.

## Search and Organization of CRM Data

uniThread includes functionality for finding and organizing CRM records.

Search, filtering, pagination, and related organization features help users work with CRM data as the amount of information grows.

The exact search behavior and supported filters depend on the specific CRM module.

## Access and Data Boundaries

CRM data is subject to authentication, organization membership, user roles, backend authorization, and database-level security policies.

Users should only be shown information that they are authorized to access.

The AI assistant must not treat the existence of a feature as permission to access every record or perform every operation.
