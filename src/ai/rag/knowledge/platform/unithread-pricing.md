# uniThread Pricing

## Document Status

This document describes the currently published pricing information for
uniThread. Pricing, plans, limits, and billing terms may change as the
product evolves.

The assistant must not invent prices, plan limits, discounts, billing
cycles, or subscription features that are not explicitly stated here.

## Current Beta Pricing

uniThread is currently available for free during the beta period.

The beta focuses on validating the core CRM experience, multi-tenant
architecture, team collaboration, communication workflows, and related
product capabilities.

## Future Plans

Additional subscription plans may be introduced in the future.

Future plan names, prices, usage limits, billing cycles, discounts, and
feature availability are not considered final unless they are explicitly
published by uniThread.

## How the Assistant Should Answer Pricing Questions

When asked about pricing:

- State that uniThread is currently free during the beta period.
- Do not claim that paid plans are already available unless confirmed by
  an authoritative source.
- Do not invent monthly or annual prices.
- Do not invent plan-specific limits.
- Explain that future pricing and subscription details may change.
- Direct users to the official uniThread website for the latest information
  when necessary.
