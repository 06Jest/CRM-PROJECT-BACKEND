# About uniThread

## Document Status

This document summarizes the official About page content for uniThread.

It describes the product's purpose, philosophy, human-centered AI principles,
developer background, vision, and mission.

Statements about future AI capabilities should not be interpreted as confirmation
that every capability is already available in the product.

## Product Overview

uniThread is a customer relationship management platform built to connect
businesses with the people they serve.

It brings customer relationships, sales, communication, and team collaboration
into one continuous thread.

The central idea behind uniThread is:

> One thread. Real connections.

The product is based on the idea that customer information and interactions
should be understood as parts of one relationship rather than disconnected
records across separate systems.

## Meaning Behind the Name

The name uniThread comes from the idea of a single thread that connects
everything together.

Within a CRM, this thread represents the relationship between:

- A lead
- A contact
- A deal
- A customer

The relationship is followed throughout the customer journey rather than treated
as separate, unrelated pieces of information.

The same thread can also connect:

- Conversations
- Activities
- Team members
- Customer information
- Communication
- Business decisions

These elements are intended to work together as parts of one customer
relationship.

## Why uniThread Was Built

uniThread is based on the belief that CRM software should help teams spend more
time building relationships and less time managing software.

Businesses may have customer information spread across different systems, with
conversations, notes, deals, activities, and team members managed in separate
places.

uniThread aims to bring these pieces together into one focused workspace.

The product's foundational values include:

- Simplicity
- Organization
- Visibility
- Collaboration
- Relationships

## Human Connection

uniThread emphasizes that customer relationship management should remain
centered on people.

A customer is not merely:

- A record in a database
- An email address
- A data point
- A source of revenue

A customer is a person.

The product's philosophy is that technology should help businesses connect with
people rather than reduce people to data records.

## Human Connection in the AI Era

uniThread recognizes that artificial intelligence is becoming part of many
business activities, including:

- Writing emails
- Generating messages
- Summarizing conversations
- Analyzing information
- Answering questions
- Automating repetitive work

As communication becomes more automated, uniThread aims to preserve genuine
human connection.

The product's position is that AI should support business users without
removing the human element from customer relationships.

## AI Philosophy

uniThread follows the principle:

> AI should assist people, not replace the relationship.

AI-powered capabilities are intended to help users:

- Write clearer emails
- Improve messages before they are sent
- Summarize customer interactions
- Organize information
- Surface important follow-ups
- Automate repetitive work
- Analyze information
- Generate suggestions and drafts

The person using uniThread remains responsible for:

- Understanding the customer
- Showing empathy
- Building trust
- Making important decisions
- Maintaining relationships
- Communicating with customers

The human remains at the center of the customer relationship.

## AI and Human Responsibilities

### AI Assists With

- Automation
- Suggestions
- Summaries
- Analysis
- Drafting
- Organization

### Humans Remain Responsible For

- Understanding
- Empathy
- Trust
- Decisions
- Relationships
- Communication

The intended relationship between AI and users is collaborative. AI can improve
productivity and help organize or interpret information, but it should not
replace human judgment, trust, or communication.

## Future AI Direction

uniThread intends to continue introducing AI-powered features and, over time,
AI agents.

The About page describes AI as a way to provide better tools for people who
manage customer relationships.

Future AI capabilities should be described carefully. The About page expresses
the product's direction and philosophy, but does not establish that every
mentioned capability is currently released or available.

For current AI feature availability, use the dedicated product-status,
AI-assistant, or roadmap knowledge documents.

## Developer Background

uniThread was created by Jestony Silvano, the developer behind the project.

Jestony graduated from Quezon City University in June 2026 with a Bachelor of
Science in Information Technology.

He graduated with Latin Honors, specifically Cum Laude, and a GPA of 1.59.

## Origin of the Project

The idea for uniThread began during Jestony's internship at Aqua Smart Guard
Corp.

During the internship, he was exposed to a real business system through
ERPNext.

That experience introduced him to how a large, interconnected software system
could support an actual business.

It led to the idea of building a smaller business-oriented system independently,
which eventually became a CRM project.

## Lessons Behind uniThread

The project helped reinforce the idea that software is more than code and
features.

Real people and businesses depend on the systems being built.

uniThread became a way for its developer to:

- Explore business software development
- Learn through mistakes
- Question technical and product decisions
- Solve problems
- Grow as a developer
- Attempt to build something genuinely useful

The project is both a product and a long-term learning experience.

## Vision

uniThread's vision is a future where businesses can use advanced technology
without sacrificing the human relationships that make those businesses
successful.

The product aims to help businesses connect with people rather than simply
treat people as sources of revenue.

The central vision is:

> The future of customer relationship management should not be human versus AI.
> It should be humans empowered by AI.

## Mission

uniThread's mission is to build a software system that creates opportunities
and measurable success between businesses and people while keeping human
connection at the center.

The mission can be summarized as:

> One thread. Real people. Better connections.
