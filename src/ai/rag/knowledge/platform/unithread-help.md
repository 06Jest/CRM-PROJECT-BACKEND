# uniThread Help Guide

## Overview

uniThread is a CRM platform for managing customer information, sales opportunities, customer relationships, tasks, notes, activities, and team collaboration.

The exact screens, actions, and available options may depend on the user's role, organization configuration, and current product version.

## Working with Leads

Leads represent potential customers or business opportunities.

Typical lead-management workflows include:

- Creating a lead
- Viewing lead information
- Updating lead details
- Assigning a lead to a team member
- Tracking lead progress
- Moving a lead through the sales workflow
- Converting or associating a lead with later CRM activity when supported

Users should verify the available fields and actions in the current Leads interface.

## Working with Contacts

Contacts represent people associated with customer relationships.

Typical contact workflows include:

- Creating a contact
- Viewing contact information
- Updating contact details
- Reviewing related notes and activities
- Associating contacts with relevant CRM records
- Searching for existing contacts

Users should only access contact information permitted by their organization and role.

## Working with Deals

Deals represent sales opportunities.

Typical deal-management workflows include:

- Creating a deal
- Associating a deal with relevant contacts or customers
- Assigning a deal to a team member
- Updating deal information
- Moving a deal through pipeline stages
- Tracking deal progress
- Reviewing related activities and notes

The available pipeline stages and actions depend on the current product configuration.

## Working with the Sales Pipeline

The sales pipeline provides a visual way to organize deals by stage.

Example stages may include:

- Prospecting
- Proposal
- Negotiation
- Closed Won
- Closed Lost

To manage a deal in a pipeline, users generally select the relevant deal and update its stage using the available interface controls.

The exact interaction may vary depending on the current interface.

## Working with Customers

Customers represent established customer relationships.

Typical customer workflows include:

- Viewing customer information
- Updating customer details
- Reviewing related contacts
- Reviewing related deals
- Reviewing notes and activities
- Maintaining customer relationship information

## Working with Tasks

Tasks represent work that needs to be completed.

Typical task workflows include:

- Creating a task
- Assigning a task to a team member
- Setting a due date
- Updating task information
- Marking a task as completed
- Reviewing pending or completed tasks

Task availability and assignment permissions depend on the user's role and organization permissions.

## Working with Notes

Notes are used to preserve useful CRM information.

Typical note workflows include:

- Creating a note
- Associating a note with a relevant CRM record
- Updating a note
- Reviewing previous notes
- Recording meeting details
- Recording customer information
- Recording follow-up context

Notes may contain sensitive business or customer information and should be handled according to the organization's policies.

## Reviewing Activities

Activities help users maintain a history of customer-related work and interactions.

Users may review activities associated with:

- Leads
- Contacts
- Deals
- Customers
- Other supported CRM records

Activity details and available activity types depend on the current product implementation.

## Using Messaging

uniThread includes messaging functionality for supported communication and collaboration workflows.

Users should distinguish between available communication channels and simulated beta functionality.

For example:

- Internal messaging may support team conversations.
- Email functionality depends on the current configuration.
- SMS may be simulated during the beta period.
- Call functionality may be simulated during the beta period.

Simulated communication should not be interpreted as a real external message or live telephone call.

## Managing Organizations

Organizations represent shared CRM workspaces.

Organization management may include:

- Viewing organization information
- Managing members
- Inviting members
- Managing roles
- Coordinating shared CRM work
- Controlling access to organization data

Only users with the required permissions should perform organization-management actions.

## Managing Members and Roles

uniThread supports organization roles such as:

- Owner
- Manager
- Agent

High-level role responsibilities may include:

### Owner

Owners may manage organization-level settings, members, billing-related configuration, and other administrative operations.

### Manager

Managers may support team management and day-to-day CRM operations.

### Agent

Agents may manage assigned CRM records and perform operations permitted by their role.

Actual permissions are determined by the application's authorization rules and may vary by feature.

## Dashboard and Metrics

The dashboard may provide information such as:

- Lead counts
- Contact counts
- Deal counts
- Customer counts
- Pipeline information
- Recent activities
- Business statistics
- Charts or summarized metrics

Dashboard information should be interpreted within the user's authorized organization context.

## Searching and Finding Records

Users can use the available search, filtering, pagination, and navigation features to find CRM information.

Search behavior may differ between CRM modules.

When searching for a record, users should verify:

- The correct module
- The record's identifying information
- The organization context
- The available filters
- The record's current status

## AI Assistant Usage

The AI assistant can help users understand supported CRM information and workflows.

Useful questions may include:

- What does uniThread do?
- What features are available?
- What are my assigned tasks?
- Summarize the available information about a lead.
- Explain how a CRM pipeline works.
- Help me understand a customer record.

The assistant's answer depends on the selected assistant, available context, retrieved information, registered tools, and user permissions.

## AI Data Access

The AI assistant must not be assumed to have access to every CRM record.

Access to private CRM information is subject to:

- Authentication
- Organization membership
- User role
- Backend authorization
- Tool authorization
- Database security policies

A user asking for information does not automatically grant permission to access it.

## AI Actions and Confirmation

Some AI-supported operations may require explicit confirmation before execution.

A confirmation request means the assistant has prepared an intended operation but has not necessarily executed it.

Users should review the proposed action and its arguments before approving it.

State-changing operations should only execute after the required confirmation and authorization checks are completed.

## When Information Is Unavailable

If the assistant cannot find enough authorized information to answer a question, it should say so clearly.

The assistant should not invent:

- CRM records
- Customer information
- Deal values
- Task status
- Product capabilities
- Organization data
- Feature availability

For current interface instructions, users should consult the relevant product screen or official help content when the available documentation is insufficient.

## Product Changes

uniThread is under active development.

Screens, workflows, feature availability, and AI capabilities may change over time.

When documentation and current application behavior differ, the current authorized application behavior and official updated documentation should take precedence.
