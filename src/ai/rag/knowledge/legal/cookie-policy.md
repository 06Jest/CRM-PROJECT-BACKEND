- Last updated: August 10, 2026

## 1. Purpose of This Policy

The Cookie Policy explains how uniThread CRM uses cookies and similar
browser-side technologies to provide a secure and reliable experience.

uniThread primarily uses these technologies to:

- Provide essential application functionality
- Maintain authenticated sessions
- Support application security
- Help maintain continuity between requests
- Remember limited preferences where applicable

The policy is intended to describe current functionality accurately. Analytics,
marketing, and other optional technologies should not be assumed to be active
unless explicitly stated.

---

## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information that a website or application can
store in a browser or on a device.

When a user returns to the application, stored information may be read back to
help the application recognize the browser and maintain continuity between
requests.

Cookies and similar technologies can help an application remember that a user
is signed in while moving between pages, rather than requiring authentication
on every request.

The policy also covers similar browser technologies, such as storage
mechanisms built into modern browsers, when they serve comparable purposes.

Unless stated otherwise, references to "cookies" may include these similar
technologies.

---

## 3. How uniThread Uses Cookies

uniThread is designed to rely on cookies and similar technologies primarily
where they are necessary for the application to function correctly and
securely.

### Authentication and Sessions

Cookies or similar browser-storage mechanisms may be used to:

- Maintain an authenticated session
- Help keep users signed in
- Preserve authentication continuity while using uniThread

### Security

Necessary technologies may help:

- Protect authenticated sessions
- Support detection of unauthorized access
- Contribute to application security
- Maintain a secure connection between the browser and application

### Application Functionality

Necessary browser-side storage may be used to retain information required for
core parts of the application to operate correctly.

### Preferences

Where applicable, and only where actually implemented, limited preference
information may be stored to remember certain settings between visits.

### No Advertising or Behavioral Tracking Purpose

The policy states that these technologies are used because they are necessary
for uniThread to operate, not for advertising or behavioral tracking.

## 4. Types of Cookies and Similar Technologies

### Strictly Necessary Technologies

**Status:** Required

These technologies may be used for:

- Authentication
- Session management
- Security
- Essential application functionality

They are considered necessary for the core operation of uniThread.

### Preference Technologies

**Status:** Optional only where implemented

These technologies may be used to remember user preferences or settings within
the application, but only where such functionality is actually implemented.

### Analytics Technologies

**Status:** Not currently used

Analytics technologies are described for completeness and may be introduced in
the future to help understand product usage.

Their inclusion in the policy does not mean that analytics functionality is
currently active.

### Marketing Technologies

**Status:** Not currently used

uniThread does not currently use cookies or similar technologies for
advertising or marketing purposes.

## 5. Authentication and Security Consequences

As a CRM that handles business and customer information, uniThread depends on
reliable authentication to protect accounts and data.

Cookies or comparable browser-side technologies may play a role in maintaining
the authenticated and secure connection between the user's browser and the
application.

Disabling or blocking necessary technologies may prevent users from:

- Signing in
- Remaining authenticated during a session
- Accessing protected CRM functionality
- Maintaining a secure session

Strictly necessary technologies cannot be turned off through in-application
settings. They can only be affected through browser controls.

---

## 6. Third-Party Technologies

uniThread may rely on third-party infrastructure and service providers for
parts of the application, including:

- Authentication
- Email delivery
- Hosting
- Other infrastructure services

The use of a third-party service does not automatically mean that the provider
places cookies in the user's browser.

The policy identifies a third-party provider as setting cookies only where that
behavior is specifically known and implemented.

uniThread does not currently identify third-party advertising or tracking
cookies as part of its operation.

---

## 7. Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow users to:

- View cookies
- Manage cookies
- Delete cookies
- Block cookies
- Adjust browser storage controls

The exact steps depend on the browser and device. Users should consult their
browser's help documentation for instructions.

### Effect of Disabling Necessary Cookies

Disabling or deleting strictly necessary cookies may affect the ability to:

- Log in
- Remain authenticated
- Maintain a session
- Use core parts of uniThread securely

Because necessary technologies support core application functions, disabling
them may affect application availability rather than merely limiting optional
features.

## 8. Cookie Consent

uniThread distinguishes between:

- Technologies necessary for core application functionality, such as
  authentication and security
- Optional technologies that may be introduced for other purposes

uniThread does not currently operate a separate cookie-consent mechanism for
strictly necessary technologies because those technologies are required for
the application to function.

If optional technologies, such as analytics or marketing cookies, are
introduced in the future, uniThread may:

- Update this Cookie Policy
- Implement appropriate consent mechanisms
- Provide choices where required by applicable law

## 12. Assistant Rules for Cookie Questions

When answering questions based on this document:

- State that uniThread primarily uses necessary technologies for
  authentication, sessions, security, and core functionality.
- Do not claim that uniThread uses analytics cookies.
- Do not claim that uniThread uses marketing or advertising cookies.
- Do not claim that a specific third-party provider sets cookies unless that
  behavior is explicitly confirmed.
- Distinguish cookies from similar browser-side storage technologies.
- Explain that disabling necessary cookies may prevent login or access to
  protected functionality.
- Do not claim that users can disable necessary cookies through an in-app
  preference panel.
- Explain that optional consent mechanisms may be introduced if optional
  technologies are added in the future.
- Refer broader personal-data questions to the Privacy Policy.
- Do not invent cookie names, expiration periods, domains, security flags,
  storage locations, consent-banner behavior, or specific providers.
