uniThread Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. About the Privacy Policy

The Privacy Policy explains how uniThread collects, uses, shares, and protects
information connected to access to and use of the Service.

The policy applies to uniThread CRM, a multi-tenant SaaS CRM and communication
platform. The platform connects people, customer relationships, business
information, communication, and teams in a shared workspace.

uniThread is currently operated personally by its developer/founder rather
than by a registered corporation. The platform is in a beta or early-stage
phase.

The policy is intended to describe what uniThread actually does. Functionality
that is limited, experimental, or simulated should be treated accordingly.

The Privacy Policy applies alongside the Terms of Service.

## 2. Information Collected

The information collected depends on how the Service is used, including
whether someone is an individual user, organization member, or organization
administrator.

### Account Information

uniThread may collect:

- Email address
- Password or authentication information
- First and last name
- Job title
- Avatar or profile image
- Account status
- Organization membership and role
- Authentication and security information

### Organization Information

uniThread may collect:

- Organization name, type, and industry
- Workspace settings
- Organization members
- Subscription plan and status
- Time zone and currency
- Invitations
- Organization identifiers

### CRM Information

Users may enter information about their customers and business relationships,
including:

- Leads
- Contacts
- Customers
- Deals
- Names
- Email addresses
- Phone numbers
- Job titles
- Statuses and deal stages
- Tags
- Notes
- Activities
- Tasks
- Communication records
- Call records
- SMS records
- Other business information entered by users

Organizations are responsible for determining the appropriate legal basis and
permissions for information they enter about their customers, employees,
leads, contacts, or business partners.

### Communication Information

uniThread may process:

- Internal messages and conversations
- Message content and metadata
- Email sender and recipient information
- Email subject and content
- Email delivery information
- SMS records
- Call records

Some SMS and call-related features may be simulated rather than connected to
a real telecommunications provider. Simulated functionality does not
represent an actual phone call or SMS delivery.

### Subscription Information

uniThread may collect:

- Subscription plan and status
- Subscription dates
- Organization associated with a subscription
- Billing-related identifiers
- Payment-related status

uniThread does not state that it stores payment-card information. Payment
processing is generally handled by a third-party payment provider where
applicable.

### Technical Information

uniThread may automatically collect:

- IP address
- Browser type
- Device type
- Operating system
- Approximate location derived from technical information
- Pages or screens visited
- Features used
- Login activity
- Authentication events
- Session information
- Error information
- Performance information
- Timestamps
- Security information
- Diagnostic information

## 5. AI Features and AI Data Processing

uniThread may contain AI-assisted functionality.

Information provided to an AI feature may be processed to generate:

- Responses
- Summaries
- Recommendations
- Classifications
- Other AI-generated results

AI-generated information may be inaccurate. Users should avoid submitting
highly sensitive personal information to AI features unless the submission is
necessary and appropriately authorized.

Where third-party AI providers are used, information may be processed by those
providers according to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

uniThread does not claim that AI-related data is never used for provider-side
training unless that restriction is technically and contractually guaranteed.

### Assistant Response Rules

When answering questions about AI privacy:

- Do not claim that AI data is never sent to third-party providers.
- Do not claim that AI providers never use data for training unless explicitly
  confirmed.
- Explain that AI processing depends on the feature and providers involved.
- Advise users not to submit highly sensitive information unless necessary and
  authorized.
- Do not imply that AI-generated results are guaranteed to be accurate.

## 6. Data Sharing

Information may be shared in the following circumstances:

### Within an Organization

Information may be shared within a user's organization according to that
organization's permissions and roles.

### Service Providers

Information may be shared with service providers necessary to operate
uniThread, including:

- Supabase, for database and authentication infrastructure
- Resend, for outbound email delivery

These providers may process data as necessary to deliver their respective
services.

### Legal Requirements

Information may be shared when required by law or legal process.

### Business Transactions

Information may be shared during a:

- Merger
- Acquisition
- Financing
- Restructuring
- Sale of assets

### User Authorization

Information may be shared when specifically authorized or requested by the
user.

uniThread does not guarantee that service providers receive no data. Providers
may process information as necessary to provide infrastructure or services.

## 7. Multi-Tenant Privacy

uniThread uses a multi-tenant architecture. Each organization has its own
workspace.

Access to organization data is intended to be restricted through a combination
of:

- Organization membership
- Authentication
- Roles
- Permissions
- Authorization controls
- Database security controls
- Row-Level Security where applicable

A user belonging to one organization should not automatically have access to
another organization's data.

uniThread uses technical and organizational measures designed to maintain
separation between organizations. This describes the intended system design
and is not an absolute guarantee that cross-organization access can never
occur.

## 8. Security

uniThread uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, which may
include:

- Authentication controls
- Authorization controls
- Role-based access control
- Organization-level data isolation
- Secure API communication
- JWT-based authentication
- Database security controls
- Row-Level Security where applicable
- Secure password handling through authentication infrastructure
- Monitoring and logging
- Backup and recovery procedures
- Restricted production access

No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

uniThread does not claim perfect or absolute security. It also does not claim
compliance with a specific security certification or standard, such as SOC 2
or PCI DSS, unless explicitly confirmed elsewhere.
